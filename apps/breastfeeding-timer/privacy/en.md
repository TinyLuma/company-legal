This policy explains how TinyLuma handles personal information. TinyLuma helps adult parents and caregivers record nursing sessions, review records, and use related quick-access features.

### 1. Information we process and why

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Nursing records | Start and end times, side, duration, and information you choose to enter | Save records and display timers and history; support synchronization when that service is enabled and used |
| Service identifiers and access information | App-generated identifiers, service user identifiers, page access results, and necessary verification information | Identify the service user, verify requests, determine accessible pages, and prevent abuse |
| Purchases and subscriptions | Products, transaction identifiers, purchase and expiration dates, trial and renewal status, and restoration results | Verify purchases, restore purchases, update access, and resolve subscription issues |
| Necessary technical information | App and OS versions, device type, request times, network information, and errors | Operate, troubleshoot, and secure the service |
| Feedback and support | Messages, contact information, and attachments you voluntarily provide | Respond to questions, handle privacy requests, and improve the service |

If the released version enables usage events or crash diagnostics, we process the corresponding feature events and diagnostic information to check functionality and investigate errors. We do not use nursing record content to build advertising profiles. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

We do not obtain your full payment card number through TinyLuma. Apple processes App Store payments. We and our subscription service provider process transaction information needed to verify subscriptions.

### 2. Device and server information

Nursing timers and records may be stored on your device. Purchase verification, page access decisions, and necessary service requests involve server processing, so not all information stays exclusively on your device.

If the released version provides and enables nursing record synchronization, using that service transmits the relevant records to the server for the synchronization functions expressly provided by that version. Restoring purchases should not be understood as restoring nursing records when synchronization is not enabled. Available functionality depends on the version and the information presented to you.

Accessible pages are determined using access results returned by the service. Free, trial, and paying users may each have access to applicable pages. Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen shortcuts, Live Activities, and Dynamic Island features are not restricted by subscription status. Their normal use may still involve processing described in this policy. Free access does not mean no data processing and does not change our privacy obligations.

### 3. Providers and other disclosures

We disclose information only as necessary to provide the service and for the purposes described here:

- **Apple** provides distribution, in-app purchases, payment processing, and subscription management under its own policies.
- **RevenueCat, when enabled,** supports purchase verification, subscription status, and purchase restoration, and may process app user identifiers and transaction information.
- **Cloudflare, when enabled,** provides backend interfaces, databases, and infrastructure and processes necessary information transmitted or stored through those services.
- **Support and legal requirements:** We may disclose necessary information to respond to your requests, comply with applicable law or valid legal process, and protect users and the service.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use nursing records to build advertising profiles. Providers may process information only as permitted by the applicable arrangements and law.

Provider policies: [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy), and [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). These links do not replace our own disclosures or responsibilities.

### 4. Retention and security

We retain information only as needed to provide the relevant service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Retention needs vary by category. Transaction and necessary audit information may not be deletable at the same time as general usage information. When handling a deletion request, we explain the applicable scope and necessary retention exceptions.

We use reasonable access controls, transmission protections, and other safeguards appropriate to the information. No system is completely secure. Protect your device and Apple account against unauthorized access to your records.

### 5. Your choices and requests

Contact the privacy address above to request access, correction, or deletion, or to ask about our processing. We may request reasonable verification information. You do not need to purchase a subscription to submit a privacy request. Other rights and appeal procedures available under applicable law remain available.

A restricted business page does not mean its records have automatically been deleted and does not prevent privacy requests. Deleting device data and deleting server data may require different actions. Uninstalling the app may not delete server data and does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple's subscription settings. Purchase restoration restores purchase relationships; it does not guarantee restoration of device records, early-user eligibility, or other free access.

You can read the privacy policy and subscription terms through text links in the subscription overlay or at the bottom of settings. “Restore Subscription” in the subscription overlay restores a purchase relationship; it is not a data deletion or export control.

### 6. Children and health-related information

TinyLuma is for adult parents and caregivers, not for independent use by children under 13. Caregivers may record information about an infant; please provide only what is needed for the recording purpose. Contact us if you believe a child has directly provided personal information without appropriate authorization.

Nursing records may include sensitive health-related information. TinyLuma is a recording tool and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. We do not infer from record content that a user must buy a particular subscription.

### 7. International processing and updates

Providers may process information outside your region. We apply necessary protections under applicable law. Contact our privacy address for information about processing relevant to your data.
